Instrument development with SCALES

I have been part of the instrument development team for SCALES (the Slicer Combined with an Array of Lenslets for Exoplanet Spectroscopy) since 2021. SCALES is a thermal infrared (2-5µm) integral field spectrograph under construction for Keck Observatory, with expected delivery in Fall 2026. I have touched every subsystem of the instrument and every phase of development, from PDR to commissioning.

Twilight observing with Keck/SCALES

Right after sunset and right before sunrise, the brightening sky makes observing impossible in the optical – but the infrared sky brightness remains (mostly) unchanged. Infrared instruments like SCALES can take advantage of often-underutilized twilight time to do high-impact science.
